WHO WE ARE

Technology with purpose.

The Computing Department focuses on developing computing knowledge, technical competence and creative problem-solving skills. It provides a learning environment where students can explore digital systems and understand how technology addresses real-world challenges.

Through theoretical learning and practical activities, students can build a foundation for further study, professional development and future opportunities in the technology industry.
